# Shop-Boss
**Shop Boss – Build Your Dream Store!**

Welcome to Shop Boss, a fun and colorful 3D shop tycoon game!

Start with a small shop, stock your shelves, serve customers and earn money to grow your business.

🛒 Build and expand your store

💰 Earn money and buy upgrades

👷 Hire staff to help you

🌍 Explore 12 unique worlds

🎨 Unlock skins and customize your character

🎯 Complete missions and grow your business

Can you become the ultimate Shop Boss?
