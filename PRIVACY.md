# Shop Boss – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Shop Boss is a 3D shop game. This policy explains what happens with your information when you play it.

## The Android app
- **No personal information is collected.** The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.
- **No in-app purchases.** All money in the game is pretend money.
- **Your progress stays on your device.** The game saves your shop in the app's own storage on your phone or tablet. It is never sent to us.
- **Device backup.** If you have turned on backups on your Android device, Android may include the game's saved progress in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Android and Google, not by Shop Boss.
- **Internet permission.** The app framework asks for the Internet permission, but the game itself does not send or receive any data over the internet.

## The web version on claude.ai
When you play the web version on claude.ai while you are signed in, your progress is also saved with your claude.ai account, so you can continue on another device. That storage is provided by Anthropic and covered by the claude.ai privacy policy. Shop Boss does not use it for anything else.

## Children
Shop Boss is suitable for children. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it also collects none from children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with a new date.
