## Full description (max 4000)
Shop Boss is a cheerful 3D shop game. Start with one banana tree and a small stand on a sunny beach, then grow it into the biggest shop in the world.

• Pick fruit, milk cows and collect eggs
• Carry products to your shelves, fridges and freezers
• Run machines that make juice, popcorn, pizza, ice cream and much more
• Serve customers at the register and grab the cash
• Hire cashiers and stockers who keep the shop running, even while you are away
• Buy upgrades: carry more, walk faster, sell for higher prices
• Open portals to 12 worlds: Sunny Beach, Snow Village, Big City, Desert Oasis, Candy Land, Fishing Harbor, Spooky Farm, Moon Base, Jungle Camp, Volcano Island, Cloud Kingdom and Coral Reef
• Serve golden VIP customers who pay triple
• Catch gift boxes that fall from the sky
• Complete missions to win 40 colors and hats, from a party hat to legendary skins like Rainbow and Fire Crown
• Watch out for surprise events like rush hour and double cash

Easy one-finger controls: drag anywhere on the screen to walk.

Plays offline. No ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. Your game saves by itself.

Available in English and Dutch.
